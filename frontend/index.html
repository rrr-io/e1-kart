WHAT IS E1?

ENGENES RACING AS ONE FOR ENHYPEN

E1, the ENHYPEN MAMA Grand Prix, is a fan-organized campaign to promote voting for ENHYPEN at the MAMA AWARDS. ENGENEs race in six F1-style teams to bring ENHYPEN to the podium.

This game is part of the fun: vote, upload your voting certificate and unlock the Interstellar track.

HOW TO PLAY

Acceleration is automatic. Phone in landscape: touch anywhere on the left half of the screen and slide left or right to steer, hold DRIFT in corners. Keyboard: ← → steer, ↓ brake, SPACE drift, E item.
